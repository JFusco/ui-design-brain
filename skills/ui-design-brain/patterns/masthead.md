## Masthead

The page-top identity band on interior pages — the page title, often with breadcrumbs and intro text, over an optional background image.

A promotional, CTA-led band that sells is a **Hero** (see `hero.md`) — a Masthead identifies the page, a Hero sells. The persistent site chrome (brand, nav, actions) is the **Header** (see `header.md`), even where publishing vocabulary calls that a masthead. An intro block that opens a section within the page — not the page-top identity band — is a **Section header** (see `section-header.md`).

**Best practices:**
- Contain the page's single h1 here — the masthead carries the page's primary title
- Keep it materially shorter than a homepage hero — a masthead orients, then yields to the content
- Keep breadcrumbs in a consistent position across page templates
- Ensure the title meets WCAG AA contrast over any background media, using a scrim or a reserved safe text zone
- Define focal-point and crop rules for authored background images so the subject survives every breakpoint
- Provide a consistent typographic fallback for the no-media case

**Common layouts:**
- Title and breadcrumbs over a full-width background image
- Text-only band with an eyebrow, h1, and intro paragraph
- Split masthead: title on the left, image on the right
- Compact article masthead with title and metadata (author, date, read time)
