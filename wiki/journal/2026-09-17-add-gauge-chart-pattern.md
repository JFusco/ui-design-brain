# Add the Gauge chart pattern

## Why
- Independent implementation and approved-specification evidence establish a reusable bounded-scale chart concept.
- Existing catalog patterns do not describe a current value against a bounded scale and qualitative ranges.

## What changed
Added Gauge chart as an aliasless canonical pattern with reduced-motion, accessible-name, threshold, and text-equivalent guidance. Added a reciprocal distinction from Pie chart. The catalog moves from 80 to 81 patterns.
