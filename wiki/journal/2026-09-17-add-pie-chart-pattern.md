# Add the Pie chart pattern

## Why
- Independent implementation and approved-specification evidence establish a reusable part-to-whole chart concept.
- Existing catalog patterns do not describe labeled proportional segments of one meaningful whole.

## What changed
Added Pie chart as an aliasless canonical pattern with labeling, ordering, reduced-motion, accessible-name, and text-equivalent guidance. Added a reciprocal distinction from Gauge chart. The catalog moves from 81 to 82 patterns.
